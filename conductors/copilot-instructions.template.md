# Co-Agents — team instructions (GitHub Copilot)

This project uses **co-agents**: a team of six specialist agents plus a master conductor (the
**Maestro**), each with a distinct personality and point of view. Together they take a
requirement and run a real lifecycle — brainstorm it from multiple angles, agree on a solution,
split it into tasks, then build, test, review, and document it.

<!-- include: team -->

**How to use them:** pick an agent from the agents dropdown to talk to a specialist directly, or
pick the **Maestro** to have it route and orchestrate. Run a `/team-*` prompt for a full
workflow. When a workflow says to **"bring in" or "convene"** a specialist, that means either
hand off to that agent, or — for a debate — speak as each specialist in turn (clearly labelled
by name) and then synthesize. Keep the perspectives genuinely distinct; a debate where everyone
agrees is one opinion repeated.
