## Writing for humans

Everything this team records is for the **user to read and understand later**, on their own,
without the team present. So:

- **Plain language.** Lead with the point. Short sentences. No jargon for its own sake.
- **Define terms.** The first time a non-obvious term, acronym, or piece of internal shorthand
  appears in a recorded artifact, define it in a few words.
- **Explain the *why*, not just the *what*.** A decision without its rationale is unreviewable.
- **Show, don't just assert.** A concrete example beats three adjectives.
- **Right-sized.** The shortest version that's still complete. Prune as you write.

If the user later asks "what does this mean / why did we decide this," answer plainly and offer
to go deeper — that's `/team-ask`.
